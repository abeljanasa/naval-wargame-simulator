# Seat card: Blue and Red players

**Your window is locked to your seat.** It shows only what your side knows. The white cell runs the clock, so your time controls are greyed out.

## The screen

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| Toolbar | Game time, speed (`paused`, `15×`…), sea state |
| Left: **Picture** | Your units and your contacts |
| Left: **Messages** | Reports and injects; the tab counts unread ones |
| Left: **Coverage** | Your sensors' coverage of your sense zones |
| Right: **Unit** | The selected unit: details, orders, engage |
| Status bar (bottom) | Refused orders in red, with the reason; map-click prompts |

## Orders (select a unit first, on the map or in Picture)

| To | Do |
|---|---|
| Plan a route | **Plan new route…**, click waypoints on the map, **Enter** to send |
| Undo a waypoint / cancel | **Backspace** / **Esc** |
| Set speed | **Ordered speed (kn)**, **Set** |
| Go silent | **Emissions** → silent (radar and jammer off) |
| Weapons control | **Weapons** → safe, tight (hostile only), free |
| Fire | **Target**, **Weapon**, **Rounds**, **Fire** |
| Patrol here | **Patrol here** (30 km round the unit); **Clear** ends the mission |
| Leave or join a group | **Detach**, or **join a group…** |

## What the unit panel tells you

- **Comms:** whether orders reach this unit. If it is *jammed*, *out of range* or *beyond the radio horizon*, your order is refused or queued. Tell the white cell what you would do.
- **Fuel:** below 30 %, a unit on a mission goes to refuel by itself.
- **Group:** which surface action group the unit belongs to; an order to the leader moves the whole group.

## Contacts

A contact is what your side **believes**: a number, a classification (from *surface contact* up to the class) and a 95 % error ellipse on the map. It may be wrong. Rings on the map are enemy weapons your sensors track: red for missiles, blue for torpedoes.

## Keys

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| **Enter** | Send the planned route |
| **Backspace** | Remove the last waypoint |
| **Esc** | Cancel the route, or deselect the unit |

## If something goes wrong

Say it out loud to the white cell and the observer. Don't restart the app: the white cell can fix most things with an override.
