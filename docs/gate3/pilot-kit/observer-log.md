**Severity:**
- **C (critical):** the exercise could not continue, or the result was wrong and misled the players.
- **M (major):** a workaround was needed, or it cost more than 5 minutes.
- **m (minor):** an irritation or a wish.

**Type:**
- **B:** bug.
- **U:** usability.
- **R:** a requirement the build does not meet.
- **D:** data (platform values, scenario).
