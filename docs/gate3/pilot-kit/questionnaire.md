Your answers are anonymous. They go into the pilot report and are used only to improve the tool.

## Part 1: System Usability Scale

The System Usability Scale (SUS; J. Brooke, 1986) is a standard ten-item questionnaire. Circle one number for each statement: 1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree. Answer every item. If you are unsure, circle 3.

| # | Statement | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |

**Scoring (for the pilot report, not the participant).**
1. For odd items, the item score is the answer − 1. For even items, it is 5 − the answer.
2. Add the ten item scores and multiply by 2.5. This gives 0–100.
3. Report the mean, the range and the number of respondents, by role.

For reference, 68 is the commonly cited average across published studies.
